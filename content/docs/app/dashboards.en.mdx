---
title: "Dashboards"
description: "Open a device's published dashboard and use its controls."
---

A dashboard is an experience created and published by the product owner in
Studio. Open a device from the **Devices** view to see its dashboard.

## Published dashboards

The app loads the published version. Studio draft changes do not appear in the
app until a new version is published.

A dashboard can include current values, charts, text, connection status,
toggles, commands, or other pages. What you see depends on the device type and
the dashboard design.

## Read and change values

Device values update when new data arrives. To control a value or command,
choose the matching control in the dashboard and wait for the result. A change
may not be applied when the device is offline or the channel is not writable.

Some dashboards also include data history, queries, or scheduling. These options
appear only when the product owner adds them to the published version.

## If the dashboard is missing

Check that the device is online and that your internet connection works. Then
reopen the device page. If there is still no dashboard, the product owner must
check the published version and its device type in Studio.
