The Flova app is your user space for connected devices. Open
`https://pwa.flova.ir/` in a browser, sign in, add a device to your account, and
open its published dashboard.

The app is currently available on the web through the Flova PWA. Use
[Console](../../console/overview) and [Studio](../../studio/overview) to create
device types and dashboards. End users use the app to claim devices and use the
published experience.

## User space and workspace

The app is your user space. Devices that you add with an activation QR code or
manual code appear in your account. The workspace in Console and Studio belongs
to the team that creates the device type, data, and dashboard.

Saving a Studio draft does not make a dashboard visible to users. The dashboard
must be published before the app can load it.
