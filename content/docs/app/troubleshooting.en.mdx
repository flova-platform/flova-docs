---
title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Work through common sign-in, activation, network, and dashboard problems."
---

## Sign-in does not finish

Check your browser and complete Flova's secure sign-in flow. If the session has
expired, sign out and sign in again. If the error continues, keep the time and
visible error message for support.

## The QR code is not scanned

Allow camera access for the browser, keep the full QR code clear and inside the
scanner, and try again. If the camera is unavailable, choose **Enter manually**
and get the activation code from Console or the product owner.

## The device does not connect to the network

For Wi-Fi setup, check that the device and browser are at the correct step:

- The browser must be connected to `Flova-Setup-*`.
- Enter the usual Wi-Fi name and password exactly.
- Keep the device powered until its reboot finishes after accepting the details.
- Return your computer or phone to the internet and wait for the status check.

For Ethernet, check the cable and the device's internet access.

## The dashboard is missing or a value does not change

Check that the device is online and your internet connection works. The app
loads only published dashboards, so the product owner must check the published
version and device type in Studio. A control also needs a writable channel, and
the device must be able to receive and apply the command.

## I do not receive notifications

Check the browser permission for `pwa.flova.ir`. If push notifications are not
available, review alerts from the **Alerts** view in the app.

For template, device, or dashboard problems, send support the device name, event
time, and visible message. Do not send activation codes, passwords, or Wi-Fi
credentials.
