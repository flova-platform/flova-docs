---
title: "Alerts and notifications"
description: "Review device alerts and manage browser notifications."
---

The **Alerts** view shows important events from your devices and automations.
Choose **Alerts** from the app navigation to open it.

Unread alerts are marked beside the Alerts section. Open an item to see its
message, device, and event time. Refresh the list to load newer items.

## Enable browser notifications

Open notification management on the Alerts page and choose **Enable
notifications**. Your browser will ask for permission. Allow it if you want
alerts while the page is closed.

If you previously blocked notifications, allow them for this site in your
browser settings and return to the app. If push notifications are unavailable
in your browser or device, alerts remain available in the app.

Browser notifications do not replace the Alerts page. Open the alert list when
you need the full message and event time.
