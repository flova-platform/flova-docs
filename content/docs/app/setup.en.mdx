---
title: "Device setup"
description: "Complete a device's network setup through the web app."
---

After you add a device, the app shows its setup state. The steps depend on the
device connection method.

## Wi-Fi setup

1. Open **Device setup** on the device page.
2. Connect your phone or computer to the network whose name starts with
   `Flova-Setup-`.
3. Return to the app and enter the name and password of your usual Wi-Fi
   network.
4. Choose **Set up device** and wait for the device to accept the details.
5. Switch your phone or computer back to its normal Wi-Fi network and the
   internet.

The app opens the device's local setup page. After the device accepts the
details, it reboots and the app checks its connection state.

## Ethernet setup

If the device is prepared for Ethernet, the app does not ask for Wi-Fi details.
Check the device cable and internet connection, then wait for its status to
update automatically.

## If setup does not finish

- Make sure the browser is still connected to `Flova-Setup-*`.
- If the local page does not open, check the browser's local network access.
- After the device accepts the details, keep it powered until the reboot ends.
- After returning to the internet, wait for the runtime status check to finish.

The app does not open a cached dashboard while the device is offline. If the
status does not change after internet access returns, see
[Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting).
