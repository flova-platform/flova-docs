The **Devices** view lists devices added to your app account. From here you can
open a device, search by name, and manage device groups when that feature is
available.

## Add a device

1. Open the [Flova app](https://pwa.flova.ir/) and sign in.
2. Choose **Add device**.
3. Scan the activation QR code on the device or its packaging with your
   browser camera.
4. If scanning is not possible, choose **Enter manually** and enter the
   activation code.
5. Name the device and choose **Confirm and activate**.

The activation code adds the device to your user account. Wi-Fi setup and the
device's later connection to Flova are separate steps.

## Find and open a device

Search by device name or filter the list by group. A device card shows its
current status and connection information. Open the card to view its
dashboard.

If the device is still being set up, complete [Device setup](../setup). An
online status only means that the device has reported recent presence. It does
not by itself prove that every channel has reported a current value.

## Manage a device

Depending on the available actions, the device menu lets you rename a device,
remove it from your account, or start a factory reset. Read the confirmation
text before removing or resetting a device. A factory reset can change the
device's network settings and connection state.

See [Devices in Console](../../console/devices) to create devices and issue
activation QR codes.
