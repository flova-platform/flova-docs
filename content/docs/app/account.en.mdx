The app needs a Flova account to show your devices and dashboards.

## Sign in

The app opens **Secure sign-in with Flova** in your browser. Complete the sign-in
there and return to the app. If sign-in does not finish, keep the browser
window open and read the displayed error.

After sign-in, devices added to your account appear in the Devices view. Team
workspace settings and device creation are managed in [Console](../../console/overview).

## Profile and sign-out

Open **Account** in the app navigation to see your display name and registered
phone number. Choose **Sign out** to end the session.

After signing out, you must sign in again to view devices and dashboards. Do not
share sign-in details or activation codes.
