title: "Quick start"
description: "Add your first device and open its published dashboard."
---

Follow these steps the first time you use the Flova app:

1. Open the [Flova app](https://pwa.flova.ir/).
2. Sign in with your Flova account.
3. Choose **Add device** and scan the activation QR code. If the camera is not
   available, enter the activation code manually.
4. Give the device a recognizable name and confirm activation.
5. If the device needs network setup, follow [Device setup](../setup).
6. Open the device and view its published dashboard.

The app only shows published dashboards. If the device is available but no
dashboard appears, ask the product owner to publish a dashboard version in
Studio.

## Before you start

- Get the device activation QR code or manual code from the product owner.
- For Wi-Fi setup, your phone or computer must be able to switch between the
  internet and the device's temporary setup network.
- An internet connection is required to load dashboards and receive current
  values.

See [Devices](../devices), [Device setup](../setup), and [Dashboards](../dashboards)
for details about each step.
