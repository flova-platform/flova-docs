custom firmware is for the team that program the device, Wi-Fi, GPIO or setup server
has its own In this model, `FlovaEsp32` and `FlovaEsp8266` are passive facades:
They don't create SoftAP, they don't replace the server, and they replace the GPIO
Your program does not control.

## Merge pattern
```text
the application owns Wi-Fi and hardware
          ↓
       FlovaSDK
          ↓
 datastream / state / command
          ↓
       FlovaLink
```
In setup, your program will bring up Wi-Fi and server. Next handoff the fluva with
Receives registered routes or another channel. After `begin`, `run` in loop
Call and register the hardware handlers before it.

## What things remain at the disposal of the program?

- Wi-Fi connection and reconnect method
- Dedicated server and program routes
- GPIO, driver and local state
- Decide on factory reset and OTA, unless you explicitly use Flova's composition

## What do you get from SDK?

- typed datastreams
- Common path of local write, remote command and automation
- Reporting the observation of the sensor
- Offline cache and policy
- Flova identity and configuration, based on the selected adapter

If your board is not ESP, use [Portable core and four services](/en/docs/sdk/custom-boards) instead of facade
use This does not force you to update the product firmware with the architecture
Rewrite universal.
